The disconnected undercut takes every element of the standard undercut and pushes it further. The sides go shorter — often a one or a zero. The top goes longer, frequently past ten centimetres. And the step between them is left completely hard, with no attempt at softening.

The result is the most deliberately artificial shape in this catalogue, and it is meant to be. Nothing about a disconnected undercut suggests it happened naturally. That is either exactly what you want or exactly what you don't.

## Who it suits

Round faces gain the most from the geometry — maximum width removed at the sides, maximum height added on top. Square faces get a hard-edged look that suits a hard-edged jaw. Oval faces can carry it.

Heart and oblong faces are poorly served. Both already carry visual weight where this cut adds it, and the effect tips into top-heavy quickly.

Hair type is the real constraint. You need thick, reasonably straight hair. The top section has to be long *and* stay where you put it, and that combination requires natural weight. Fine hair cannot hold the length. Curly hair can, but the volume on top becomes disproportionate to the bare sides.

## Getting the cut right

Ask for a hard line explicitly. Many barbers will instinctively soften a disconnection because a blurred version grows out better, and if you want the graphic version you need to say so.

Also agree how long the top will be before they start. This cut lives or dies on proportion, and the difference between eight and twelve centimetres on top is the difference between striking and unwieldy.

## Styling it day to day

There is no low-effort version. A long top over short sides has no structural support, so it has to be dried into shape every time. A round brush, heat, and direction — back and up — are the routine.

Use a firm hold clay rather than a cream. You need real hold here, and matte finish rather than shine, since gloss on a disconnected undercut reads as very dated. Once set, break the surface with your fingers so it moves.

## Living with it

Two weeks on the sides. The hard line is the whole feature and it blurs fast. A disconnected undercut with soft sides is a shapeless haircut — there is no intermediate state that works.

The top, as with any undercut, can go months. Budget for frequent short appointments rather than infrequent long ones, and the cut becomes manageable.
