A curly fringe leaves the front long enough that the curls fall forward over the forehead, with the sides tapered underneath. It is the curly-haired equivalent of a textured crop, and it works for the same reason: the fringe covers the hairline entirely and puts the emphasis on texture.

The single thing that determines whether it works is length, and the single reason it often goes wrong is shrinkage. Wet curls are twice as long as dry ones, sometimes more, so a fringe that looks right when cut wet ends up sitting well above the eyebrows once it dries.

## Who it suits

Oblong and long faces gain most — the fringe shortens the face and the curl adds width. Square faces get softened. Heart faces benefit from the forehead coverage.

Oval faces have no issue. Round faces are the weakest fit, since a fringe adds horizontal at the forehead while the curl adds width at the sides. A curly top with height is the better call there.

Curly and thick wavy hair suit it naturally. Coily hair works with the fringe worn shorter, since tighter coils shrink more and hang less.

## Getting the cut right

Ask to be cut dry. This matters more for a curly fringe than for almost any other cut, because the fringe length is the entire shape and shrinkage makes it unpredictable when cut wet.

Ask for the fringe to sit at or just above the eyebrow *when dry*. Say the words "when dry" — it removes all ambiguity.

Sides tapered rather than faded keeps the look soft; a fade underneath gives a sharper, more modern version. Both work.

## Styling it day to day

Curl cream on soaking wet hair, scrunch the fringe upward once, then leave it alone entirely until dry. Scrunching upward rather than letting the fringe hang encourages it to sit higher, which keeps it out of your eyes.

Diffuse on low if you need it dry quickly. On the second day, spray the fringe with water and re-scrunch — the front is what people see, and refreshing it alone takes thirty seconds.

## Living with it

Four to five weeks. The fringe is the variable: a couple of centimetres of growth takes it from sitting on the forehead to sitting in your eyes, and the balance of the cut shifts with it.

As with any curly cut, moisture matters more than styling. Dry curls lose their definition regardless of how well the fringe was cut.
