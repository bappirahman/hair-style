A curly top keeps full length and volume on top while the sides are taken short and tapered. It is the most straightforward way to build a haircut around natural curl rather than in spite of it, and it is one of the most requested shapes for curly hair for good reason: the contrast between tight sides and dense curl above is immediate and unmistakable.

The cut itself is simple. What makes it work is how it is cut, and what happens in the shower afterwards.

## Who it suits

Oblong and long faces gain from the width that curl volume adds at the sides of the crown. Square faces are softened by the round shape. Heart and diamond faces benefit from volume that sits above rather than at the widest part of the face.

Oval faces carry it easily. Round faces are the one to be careful with — a round mass of curl above a round face compounds it, and a taller, narrower curly shape or a curly fade serves better.

Curly, coily and thick wavy hair are what this cut is for. There is no version for straight hair, and no product creates a convincing curl pattern that is not there.

## Getting the cut right

Ask to be cut dry. Curls shrink dramatically as they dry — often by half — so hair cut wet ends up shorter and shaped differently from what the barber saw. A curl specialist will cut dry as a matter of course; a general barber may need to be asked.

Say no to thinning shears. Thinning curly hair creates short pieces that spring out of the curl pattern as frizz. Volume in curly hair is controlled by shape and length, not by removing internal bulk.

Ask for the sides to be tapered rather than faded to skin unless you want the higher-contrast look. A taper keeps some softness against the curl above.

## Styling it day to day

Apply curl cream or leave-in conditioner to soaking wet hair, in the shower. Product distributed through damp hair coats unevenly; product distributed through wet hair reaches every strand.

Scrunch upward once, then leave it alone. Every time you touch a curl while it dries you disrupt the pattern, and disrupted curl is exactly what frizz is.

Dry with a microfibre towel or a cotton t-shirt rather than a terry towel, and use a diffuser on low heat if you need it dry quickly. Never brush dry curls.

## Living with it

Four to five weeks on the sides. The top can go considerably longer — many people find their curls sit best a month or two after a cut.

The genuine daily commitment is moisture rather than styling. Curly hair is structurally prone to dryness because the natural oils cannot travel down a coiled shaft, and condition is what determines whether curls look defined or dull.
