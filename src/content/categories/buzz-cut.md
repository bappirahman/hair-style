Buzz cuts remove framing entirely. There is no gradient, no parting and no styling — the hair is one short length, or very close to it, and what you see is the actual shape of the head underneath.

That honesty is the appeal and the risk. On a well-proportioned skull with a defined jaw, a buzz cut is one of the most flattering things available. On a round face with no facial hair, it removes the two things that were doing the work.

The family runs by length. An **induction cut** uses no guard at all and leaves a shadow. A **buzz cut** typically means a one, two or three guard — the two is the standard and the most forgiving. A **crew cut** graduates rather than staying uniform, keeping a little more at the front so there is something to style.

All three are among the lowest-maintenance cuts in this catalogue, and all three shift the visual weight to your face, which is why most people who wear them pair them with stubble or a beard.
