Medium length is where hairstyles start being choices rather than cuts. Below this, the shape is determined by the clippers. Here, the same length of hair can be swept back, parted centrally, pushed forward or left loose, and each reads completely differently.

The practical threshold is around eight centimetres on top — enough to reach the crown when swept back, enough to part, enough to fall. Below that you have a short cut; above about fifteen you are into long hair territory and the rules change again.

The **quiff** and **modern pompadour** build volume at the front. The **slick back** and **medium swept back** move everything in one direction. **Curtains** and the **middle part** divide it centrally. **Flow** grows it past the ears and tucks it behind them. The **undercut** family keeps medium length on top over short sides.

What they share is that the sides need less attention than a fade and the top needs more attention than a buzz. Most medium styles run on a five-to-eight-week cutting cycle with two to three minutes of daily styling.
