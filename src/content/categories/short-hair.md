Short hair covers most of what walks into a barbershop. It is defined less by a single shape than by a shared property: the cut does the work, so the styling doesn't have to.

That trade is the useful way to choose within it. A **buzz cut** or **induction cut** removes styling entirely and puts everything on your head shape. A **low taper fade** makes hair look cut with the smallest possible change and lasts six weeks. A **French crop** or **textured crop** covers the hairline and needs two minutes a day. A **high fade** or **skin fade** gives maximum contrast and demands a fortnightly appointment.

Short does not mean uniform. The gap between a skin fade and a classic taper is larger than the gap between many medium-length styles, both in how they look and in what they cost you weekly.

If you are choosing on maintenance alone, the ranking runs roughly: buzz cut, low taper, classic taper, crop, low fade, mid fade, high fade, skin fade.
